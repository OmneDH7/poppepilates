Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Poppe Pilates (“we,” “us,” or “I”), operated by Camille Poppe, collects and uses information when you visit poppepilates.com or contact us through this website.

Information we collect

If you use the contact form, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Type of inquiry (for example, private session or brand event)
  • Message contents

We do not ask for payment details, medical records, or other sensitive personal information through this form.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Respond to your inquiry
  • Schedule or discuss sessions, events, or corporate offerings
  • Follow up about services you requested

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use the contact form to add you to a marketing list unless you clearly ask us to stay in touch for that purpose.

How your information is processed

The website contact form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service that emails your submission to camille@poppepilates.com. FormSubmit may process your submission according to its own privacy practices.

Our website is hosted on infrastructure provided by our hosting provider. Server logs may include technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, which are used for security and site reliability.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated.

How long we keep information

Contact form messages are kept only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and manage any related booking or follow-up, unless a longer retention period is required for legal or operational reasons.

Your choices

You may email camille@poppepilates.com to ask questions about your information, request a correction, or ask us to delete a contact inquiry we no longer need to keep.

Children

This website is intended for adults inquiring about Pilates services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the contact form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version.
