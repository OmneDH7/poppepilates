The practice

Benefits of Pilates

Pilates strengthens the body as a whole and helps you move better in everyday life, while also supporting performance and balance in other sports.

  • Improve posture
  • Build core and full-body strength
  • Increase mobility and flexibility
  • Improve balance and coordination
  • Develop body awareness and movement quality
  • Support physical and mental health

Whether you’re new to Pilates or have years of experience, every session is designed around your goals, movement patterns, and lifestyle. Individual and duo sessions available in your home or building gym.

Questions

FAQ

Where does Poppe Pilates teach?

Camille offers private Pilates in Lower Manhattan, in your home or building gym, and online.

What is a private Pilates session with Camille?

A private session is one-on-one attention tailored to your goals, energy, and form. Sessions are supportive and challenging, with real-time guidance to keep intensity appropriate and alignment safe.

Does Poppe Pilates offer events or company sessions?

Yes. Camille organizes mat-based Pilates for brand and marketing events, and onsite sessions for offices and companies as a one-time event or a recurring class. No equipment is required from the host.

How do I book a session?

Send a direct message on Instagram at @poppepilates or use the contact form. Inquiries go to camille@poppepilates.com.
