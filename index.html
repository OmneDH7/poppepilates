Meet Camille

Camille Poppe

Private Pilates Instructor

Through my 15 years of personal Pilates practice, I've experienced how powerful mindful movement can be for building strength, improving posture, moving with greater ease, and feeling more confident and balanced in your body.

As a certified Pilates instructor, I create sessions that meet you where you are, while challenging you to build strength, confidence, and a deeper awareness of your body.

One-on-one

A private session gives you focused attention and a practice built entirely around you — so you progress with confidence, clarity, and care.

Reaching your daily potential

Your session adapts to your energy and needs each day, allowing you to get the most out of your practice.

Gentle intensity

Build strength and stability through controlled, low-impact movements designed for your body.

Reducing injury risk

The practice

Benefits of Pilates

Pilates strengthens the body as a whole and helps you move better in everyday life, while also supporting performance and balance in other sports.

  • Improve posture
  • Build core and full-body strength
  • Increase mobility and flexibility
  • Improve balance and coordination
  • Develop body awareness and movement quality

Whether you’re new to Pilates or have years of experience, every session is designed around your goals, movement patterns, and lifestyle. Individual and duo sessions available in your home or building gym.
